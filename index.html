    Sustainability & CSR

    Every tonne of cooling, balanced with trees.

    Cooling uses energy, and the equipment behind it carries embodied carbon. We take responsibility for both on the projects we deliver.

    Our tree commitment

    One tree for every TR we install.

    For every ton of refrigeration (TR) of cooling capacity we install, Infrigidate plants a tree. We also plant additional trees to offset the embodied carbon of the installed system: the emissions from manufacturing its equipment, ductwork and piping.

    The programme began with our projects for Envalior India and Jotun India, and now applies to every HVAC system we deliver.

    1. 1 TROne treefor each ton of refrigeration installed
    2. + CO₂eEquivalent treesfor the system's embodied carbon
    3. 140 TROffset so farof installed cooling balanced with trees, from Envalior and Jotun onwards
    Efficiency by design

    The cleanest kilowatt is the one a building never uses.

    Most of a system's carbon comes from running it. Our design and commissioning choices aim at lifecycle energy, not just first cost.

    • Equipment selected on lifecycle cost and part-load efficiency
    • Simulation to confirm conditions and avoid oversized plant
    • Energy management (EMS & IoT) to monitor and cut consumption
    • Thorough commissioning so systems run as designed
    • Retrofit studies and energy audits for existing facilities
