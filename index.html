    Our story

    Bridging design intent and site reality.

    Infrigidate Engineers India Pvt. Ltd. was established in 2024 in Pune by professionals from high-tech HVAC infrastructure projects. We have since extended operations to Madhya Pradesh with an office at Pithampur, and today a team of 25+ professionals delivers projects across India.
